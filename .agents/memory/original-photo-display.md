---
name: Original photo display
description: Why Timeline and its slideshow should prioritize untouched camera photos.
---

Show the original camera photos without enhancement effects unless the user
explicitly requests a new enhancement mode.

**Why:** The user rejected the enhancement experiments because the images
looked too dark, then withdrew the requested AI-editing replacement and asked
to see the originals instead.

**How to apply:** Do not treat earlier enhancement plans as permission to add
OpenCV, gamma, brightness filters, or generative edits back into Timeline or
the slideshow. Retain the requested photo count and playback duration.
