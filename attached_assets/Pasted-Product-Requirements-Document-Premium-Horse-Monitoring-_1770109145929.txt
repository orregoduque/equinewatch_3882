Product Requirements Document: Premium Horse Monitoring System Landing Page
1. Product Overview
A landing page for an AI-powered, portable monitoring device that uses computer vision and machine learning to detect behavioral anomalies in stabled horses and alert owners to potential health or welfare issues.
2. Target Audience
Primary:
	∙	Professional equestrians (show jumpers, dressers, eventers)
	∙	Horse owners with high-value animals
	∙	Breeding farms and stud operations
	∙	Equine rehabilitation centers
Secondary:
	∙	Riding schools with valuable school horses
	∙	Veterinary clinics offering monitoring services
	∙	Racing stables
3. Key Value Propositions
	∙	24/7 peace of mind - continuous monitoring without physical presence
	∙	Early problem detection - catches colic, lameness, distress before they escalate
	∙	Portable & easy setup - no permanent installation required
	∙	Smart alerts - ML reduces false alarms, only notifies when truly needed
	∙	Veterinary-grade insights - actionable information for horse health decisions
4. Page Structure & Content Sections
4.1 Hero Section
Goal: Immediately communicate what the product does and for whom
	∙	Headline: “Never Miss a Warning Sign — AI-Powered Monitoring for Your Horse’s Health”
	∙	Subheadline: “Portable camera system with intelligent alerts when your horse needs attention”
	∙	Hero visual: High-quality image/video of device mounted in stall with happy, healthy horse
	∙	Primary CTA: “Get Early Access” or “Request Demo”
	∙	Secondary CTA: “See How It Works” (scroll to video)
	∙	Trust indicators: “Trusted by 500+ horse owners” / “Veterinarian approved”
4.2 Problem Statement
Goal: Establish emotional connection and validate the need
	∙	Brief section acknowledging owner concerns: late-night colic, undetected injuries, behavior changes while away
	∙	Statistics on preventable horse health issues
	∙	Visual: Split image showing worried owner vs. peaceful monitoring
4.3 How It Works
Goal: Demystify the technology, show simplicity
Three-step process:
	1.	Install in minutes - Portable mount, no wiring needed
	2.	AI watches 24/7 - Captures images, analyzes behavior patterns
	3.	Get smart alerts - Notification when anomalies detected
Include: Product demo video (60-90 seconds), technical specs in expandable section
4.4 Key Features
Goal: Address specific pain points with product capabilities
Feature cards with icons:
	∙	Behavioral analysis - Detects pacing, lying down abnormally, distress signals
	∙	Night vision - Clear monitoring in complete darkness
	∙	Portable design - Move between stalls, take to shows/events
	∙	Smart notifications - Mobile alerts with image evidence
	∙	Historical tracking - Pattern recognition over time
	∙	Multi-horse management - Monitor entire stable from one dashboard
	∙	Battery backup - Continues working during power outages
	∙	Privacy-first - Your data stays secure
4.5 Use Cases / Scenarios
Goal: Help prospects see themselves using the product
3-4 scenario cards:
	∙	Post-surgery recovery - Monitor healing horse overnight
	∙	Competition travel - Bring peace of mind to away shows
	∙	Foaling season - Never miss critical moments
	∙	Senior horse care - Extra vigilance for aging companions
4.6 Technology Trust Section
Goal: Build credibility around the AI/ML claims
	∙	Explanation of ML model training (trained on X hours of equine behavior)
	∙	Veterinary partnerships or advisory board
	∙	Accuracy metrics if available
	∙	Image showing sample alert with explanation
4.7 Social Proof
Goal: Leverage customer validation
	∙	Testimonials - 3-4 horse owner quotes with photos
	∙	Case study - One detailed success story (e.g., “Caught early colic signs at 2 AM”)
	∙	Logos - If used by notable stables/organizations
	∙	User-generated content - Real photos of devices in action
4.8 Comparison / Alternatives
Goal: Position against competition and manual monitoring
Simple comparison table:
	∙	Manual barn checks vs. Standard cameras vs. Our system
	∙	Highlight: 24/7 coverage, intelligent alerts, portability, no false alarms
4.9 Pricing Preview (Optional)
Goal: Set expectations, qualify leads
	∙	Transparent pricing tiers or “Starting at $XXX/month”
	∙	What’s included in each tier
	∙	Financing options if available
	∙	Money-back guarantee
4.10 FAQ Section
Goal: Address common objections and questions
Questions like:
	∙	How is this different from a regular security camera?
	∙	What behaviors can it detect?
	∙	How long does battery last?
	∙	Will it work in my barn setup?
	∙	What happens if my internet goes down?
	∙	Can multiple people receive alerts?
	∙	Is there a subscription fee?
4.11 Final CTA Section
Goal: Drive conversion
	∙	Compelling headline: “Give Your Horse the Protection They Deserve”
	∙	Primary CTA: “Pre-Order Now” or “Start Free Trial”
	∙	Risk reversal: 30-day money-back guarantee
	∙	Urgency if applicable: Limited launch pricing, waitlist spots
4.12 Footer
	∙	Company information
	∙	Contact details
	∙	Links: Privacy policy, Terms of service, Support
	∙	Social media links
	∙	Newsletter signup
5. Design Principles
Visual Identity:
	∙	Premium, trustworthy aesthetic (not cheap tech gadget)
	∙	Equestrian-inspired color palette (natural tones, possibly navy/hunter green)
	∙	High-quality horse photography throughout
	∙	Clean, modern layout with ample white space
Photography/Video:
	∙	Real horses in authentic stable settings
	∙	Device shown in context (mounted, in use)
	∙	Happy horse owners with their animals
	∙	Mix of lifestyle and product shots
Tone of Voice:
	∙	Professional but warm
	∙	Knowledgeable about horses (use correct terminology)
	∙	Reassuring and confident
	∙	Avoid over-technical jargon in main copy
6. Technical Requirements
Performance:
	∙	Mobile-first responsive design
	∙	Page load time under 3 seconds
	∙	Optimized images (WebP format)
	∙	Smooth scroll animations
Integrations:
	∙	Email capture form (connect to CRM/email service)
	∙	Analytics tracking (conversion events)
	∙	Live chat widget for sales questions
	∙	Video hosting (YouTube/Vimeo embed)
SEO Considerations:
	∙	Meta title/description optimized for “horse monitoring system”
	∙	Structured data markup for product
	∙	Alt text on all images
	∙	Clear heading hierarchy
7. Success Metrics
	∙	Conversion rate (visitor to lead/purchase)
	∙	Email capture rate
	∙	Video engagement rate
	∙	Time on page
	∙	Scroll depth
	∙	CTA click-through rates
	∙	Bounce rate
